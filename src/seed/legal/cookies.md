This page explains what the StumpNote website (stumpnote.com) stores in your browser. The StumpNote web app at app.stumpnote.com is covered by the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), section 13.

## The short version

- The website sets **no advertising cookies** and no cross-site tracking cookies. It loads no Google Analytics and no advertising tags.
- The only cookie the website ever sets is a **sign-in session cookie for staff** who log in to the site's admin area. It is strictly necessary for that sign-in and is not set for ordinary visitors.
- If we measure visits to this website, we use a privacy-friendly, cookieless method that counts visits in aggregate without storing an identifier on your device. {{LEGAL_REVIEW: name the analytics provider and confirm it runs in cookieless mode before this sentence is published.}}

## What is stored on your device

| Item | Type | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theme choice | Browser storage (local storage) | Remembers light or dark if you pick one | Until you clear site data |
| Motion choice | Browser storage (local storage) | Remembers whether you turned animations on or off | Until you clear site data |
| Staff session | Cookie, staff only | Keeps a staff member signed in to the admin area | Short session, ended on sign-out |

None of these are shared with other companies, and none are used to identify you.

## Hosting logs

Our hosting provider records standard request information (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for how logs are handled.

## Your choices

You can clear site data or block cookies in your browser at any time. The website still works; your theme and motion choices will simply reset.
