You can delete your StumpNote account and its data yourself, inside the app. This page explains how, and what happens next. The full detail is in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), section 9.

## Before you start

- **Cancel any subscription first.** Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it with Apple (iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, StumpNote) or with Google Play before you delete. See [Support](/support).
- If you are a coach or a team manager, tell your team first: your notes and team links are removed with your account.

## How to delete your account

- **StumpNote (player):** {{DELETE_ACCOUNT_PATH}}, then confirm.
- **StumpNote Coach:** Home, Account, Delete account.
- **StumpNote Parent:** Settings, Delete account.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile and the content you created, including journal entries, transcripts, goals, plans, and the AI memory built from them. Deletion is immediate in our active systems, and cleanup is completed {{RETENTION_PERIOD}}.
